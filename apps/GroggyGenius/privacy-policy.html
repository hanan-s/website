Groggy Genius Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Groggy Genius has no accounts. Everything you enter in the app, including your name, decks, cards, and alarms, is stored and processed on your device. The only information that leaves your device is what is needed to manage a Pro subscription, handled by Apple and our subscription provider, RevenueCat.

This policy is published by Hanan Salim ("we", "us"), based in the United States. It applies to the Groggy Genius app for iPhone.

What the app does

Groggy Genius is an alarm clock that you turn off by reviewing flashcards. You create your own decks, add or import cards, and set alarms. To dismiss an alarm, you review cards from your chosen deck and grade how well you remembered each one. The app schedules future reviews using spaced repetition and shows your progress and streaks.

Information stored only on your device

The following stays on your iPhone. We do not receive it, and it is not sent to any server:

  • The name you'd like to be called and the occupation you choose during setup
  • Decks and flashcards you create or import
  • Alarms, their times, repeat days, and linked decks
  • Review history, grades, streaks, and progress statistics
  • App preferences and settings

Importing cards from a file. If you import flashcards from a CSV file, the app reads that file on your device only. The file is not uploaded anywhere.

Device backups. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud Backup or to a computer, iOS may include this app data in that backup. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple under Apple's terms, not by us.

Information we do not collect

We do not receive or collect:

  • Your name (the name you enter during setup never leaves your device)
  • Your email address, phone number, or other contact details
  • Your location
  • Contacts, photos, health data, or microphone or camera input
  • The content of your decks and flashcards
  • Advertising identifiers

The app has no user accounts and no sign-in. It contains no advertising.

Subscriptions and purchases

Groggy Genius offers an optional Pro subscription (monthly or annual), which may include a one-week free trial. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, Apple charges the subscription price and renews it automatically until you cancel.

Apple processes all payments through your Apple Account. We never see your payment card details, name, or Apple Account email. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your iPhone's Settings under your Apple Account > Subscriptions.

RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) is the service we use to check whether you have an active subscription. When the app starts, and when you buy or restore a purchase, RevenueCat receives:

  • A random, anonymous identifier created for your install of the app. It is not linked to your name, email, or Apple Account.
  • Your purchase and subscription history for this app: which plan, trial status, purchase and renewal dates, and transaction identifiers from Apple
  • Basic technical details needed to process the request, such as app version, iOS version, device model, language and region settings, and your IP address
  • When you view the subscription screen, basic events such as whether it was opened, closed, or led to a purchase

We use this information only to unlock Pro features, restore purchases, provide subscription support, and understand how the subscription screen performs. RevenueCat processes it on our behalf under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

Alarms. The app asks for permission to schedule alarms through Apple's AlarmKit, so alarms can ring in Silent mode and Focus. Alarm scheduling happens on your device, and no alarm data is sent to us. You can change this permission at any time in the Settings app.

The app does not use push notifications from a server.

Analytics and crash reports

The app does not contain any analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or attribution tools.

If you have chosen, in your iPhone's settings, to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with crash reports and aggregated usage statistics. Apple controls this data collection under its own privacy policy. The data does not identify you to us, and you can turn it off in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

How we share information

We do not sell your information. We do not share it for advertising.

The only parties that receive information through the app are:

  • Apple, to process purchases and, if you opt in, to provide crash and usage statistics
  • RevenueCat, to manage subscriptions, as described above

We may also disclose information if required by law.

Tracking

Groggy Genius does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. It does not use your device's advertising identifier, and it does not share data with data brokers or ad networks.

Data retention

Data on your device stays there until you delete it in the app or delete the app.

RevenueCat keeps subscription records for as long as needed to provide the subscription, meet legal and accounting obligations, and resolve disputes. You can ask us to delete them at any time (see below).

Deleting your data

  • On your device: Delete individual decks, cards, or alarms in the app. To delete everything at once, delete the app from your iPhone. This permanently removes all of its data from the device.
  • Subscription records: Contact us through our support page to ask us to delete your subscription records held by RevenueCat. Because the app has no accounts, please include the Apple order ID from your App Store receipt email so we can find your record. Deleting these records does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel it through Apple first.

Children

Groggy Genius is a general-audience app. It is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will delete it.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct it
  • Delete it
  • Object to or restrict how we use it
  • Receive a copy of it in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent, where we rely on consent
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights

To use any of these rights, contact us through our support page. We respond within 30 days, or sooner if the law requires.

EU, EEA, and UK users. Hanan Salim is the controller of the limited personal information described above. We use subscription data to perform our contract with you (providing the Pro subscription) and for our legitimate interest in running and improving the app. Everything you enter in the app is processed on your device. Security

Data on your device is protected by iOS security features, including device encryption when a passcode is set. Information sent to RevenueCat is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we limit what we collect to reduce risk.

Changes to this policy

If we add features that change how the app handles data, we will update this policy before those features are released. We will change the effective date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or in the App Store release notes. Continuing to use the app after an update means the updated policy applies.
